# Design system

Current direction: a personal website with a quiet nocturnal character throughout. The owner rejected the first draft's generic portfolio composition and chose to remove the standalone Night page. This document supersedes that draft's art direction; the verified content and accessible behavior remain requirements.

- Scene: a personal page being read at a desk in the evening, with papers and working software nearby. The background is dark green, not a separate themed destination.
- Background #14201d, text #e5e9df, secondary text #a9b8ae, rules #35483e, accent #dfc7a4, inset surface #1a2a24. Accent color is used sparingly for reading hierarchy.
- Retain self-hosted Manrope and system Chinese fonts. Ordinary reading scale: 16 px body, 34 to 44 px page headings, a maximum 52 px name on the homepage. No oversized split name, slogan typography, or repeated uppercase section labels.
- The homepage is a short first-person introduction, a work index, and a compact publication list. The only opening image is an actual Singularity screenshot linking to the application.
- Projects are text-first entries with explicit contributions and source links. Medit and Singularity have actual screenshots. Projects without screenshots use text, not fabricated interfaces or decorative logo tiles.
- Remove generic slogans, repeated promotional contact banners, invented personal anecdotes, simulated handwriting, and the generated landscape. Do not use a visible explanation of atmosphere as a substitute for atmosphere.
- Native document scrolling, explicit language paths, accessible focus, keyboard navigation, recoverable copying, and reduced-motion behavior stay intact. Mobile navigation works without JavaScript.
- Seven bilingual destinations: Home, Research, Projects, About, CV, Contact, and Prompts. No Night page, navigation item, alternate-locale entry, or sitemap URL.
- Paper titles remain in their original language. Original English prompt bodies remain exact. Education and professional claims use existing owner-authored data and the content evidence ledger.
- Print styles use white paper and dark text. The sharing image reuses the current homepage introduction and application screenshot.

The revised source is the current local draft for owner review. Technical checks establish behavior and readability; they do not establish that a design feels personal to its owner.
