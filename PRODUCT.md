# Zibin Zhao: an interactive archive

Canonical product definition for the September 8, 2026 local archive trial. Nine independently selectable leaves now carry real work and open a reader on the homepage. This content-bearing archive supersedes the previous blank-leaf kinetic trial and earlier field compositions.

## Purpose

A personal research and product profile that visitors can explore through its actual work. The orange archive is a way to select and read papers and projects. Each physical leaf has an identity, a printed title, and a corresponding reading state.

## Experience

The homepage presents nine curved, articulated leaves around visible metal hardware. Hover identifies a leaf; selecting it swings that leaf into a reading position while its neighbors move aside. The same page displays its title, source information, full description or bibliographic record, and real destination links.

Visitors can also open the collection through a native button, move through all nine entries with Previous and Next, or choose any entry in a nine-button index. Paging wraps at either end. Escape or the close button returns to the collection and restores focus to the collection launcher. Arrow keys page through entries while focus is within the reader.

The nominal 3.3-second camera opening remains interruptible. Dragging, another-angle, reset, whole-instrument open/close, and pause/resume controls remain available. Reduced motion skips the camera opening and starts still; selecting a leaf and reading every entry must continue to work without enabling ambient motion.

Eight native destination tags still lead directly to Research, Projects, Singularity, Medit, About, Prompts, CV, and Contact. The site Index reaches all seven portfolio pages at every viewport size. Portrait layouts combine a large archive opening, an adapted same-page reader, and a continuous scrollable collection. Interior routes retain their floating reading sheets over the fixed backdrop.

## Observable checks

- Every rendered leaf selects its matching real record. Selection changes that leaf's pose; the reader and active index agree on the current entry.
- All nine entries are reachable through native controls, with working Previous/Next, direct selection, source links, Escape, and focus restoration.
- Reduced motion and pause preserve selection and reading while suppressing continuous ambient motion. Hidden and offscreen scenes suspend continuous rendering.
- WebGL failure or context loss leaves the fallback, eight native destination tags, and site Index usable. The enhanced archive reader and inoperative scene controls are hidden; all source material remains available through the portfolio routes.
- Mobile, 320 px, enlarged text, and landscape resizing preserve readable content, reachable controls, vertical scrolling, and freedom from horizontal overflow.
- Existing locale navigation, skip links, project filters, prompt copy/recovery, hash navigation, downloads, metadata, sitemap, and 404 behavior remain intact.
- Inspect desktop and portrait archive states and run relevant static and browser checks. Record actual results in [the archive verification receipt](docs/archive-verification.md).
